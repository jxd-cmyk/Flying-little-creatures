# Flying-little-creatures

* What the project does

The Flying-little-creatures (sub)project serves to provide 
inspiration and creative potential to computer users who
are mindlessly staring at their monitors.

* Why the project is useful

The project is useful because dementia is the greatest 
threat of the 21st century. Plus, both project operators
enjoy it, and it might be fun for you, too.

* How can users get started with our project?

The first (passive) form of collaboration involves
downloading one of our versions of the 
Flying-little-creatures app to your computer, launching it,
and watching the dancing little creatures until a wave of
creativity strikes, which will enable this project to grow.
At that moment, the second (active) form of collaboration
begins. This can take many forms. 
Improving and expanding the code, acquiring and adding
sets of photos of creatures that aren’t yet dancing in the
Flying-little-creatures project documentation, or sending
financial support to the project’s authors.

* Where can users get help with our project?

Usually nowhere, but you can try to get advice by sending
a specific question to the project’s lead penguin 
(jxd-cmyk)
